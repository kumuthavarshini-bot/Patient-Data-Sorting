# Persona: customer data operations analyst

**Source status:** Persona synthesized from the BRD, not a research-validated person.

The analyst prepares customer records from external systems and wants to import accurate data without spending hours on manual spreadsheet cleanup. Needs include explainable mapping suggestions, clear validation summaries, record-level actionable errors, correction and reprocessing, and assurance that invalid records cannot enter an import. Validate these needs with participants before treating this persona as representative.
