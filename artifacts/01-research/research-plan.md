# Research plan

**Status:** Planned; no interviews or usability sessions have been performed in this repository.

1. Interview data operations analysts and data stewards about source formats, correction workflows, risk decisions, and current time costs.
2. Collect consented, de-identified representative CSV schemas and error categories; do not collect production PII into this repo.
3. Validate mapping review, exception correction, and import-preview concepts with representative tasks.
4. Measure task completion, critical error rate, mapping corrections, confidence, and preparation time with agreed baseline/method.

Recruitment, consent, sample sizes, and data handling require owner approval.
