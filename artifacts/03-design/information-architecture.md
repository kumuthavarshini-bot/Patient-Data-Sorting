# Information architecture

- **AI Orchestration dashboard:** seven product-planning phases, gates, and separate five-agent overview.
- **Live Demo:** browser-local CSV intake, preview, profiling, mapping, normalization, validation, exception/output views, and session-only chatbot.
- **UX artifacts library:** problem, research plan/evidence, discovery, design, implementation, evaluation, and final review files.
- **Runtime evidence:** local `runs/<run-id>/` manifests, event stream, agent results, and downloadable artifacts.

Seven product-planning phases, seven CSV-processing stages, and the additional ten-stage UX evidence workflow are distinct concepts.
