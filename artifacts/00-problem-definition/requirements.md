# Product requirements source

Use the BRD `docs/brd-analysis.md` and `input/customer_data_cleaner_BRD.md` as authoritative. Core requirements: upload and profile CSV; suggest and confirm mappings; normalize only approved formats; preserve source values and transformation history; deterministically validate; separate eligible and invalid rows; provide traceable exception exports; require preview and explicit confirmation before import; record outcomes. Proposed success metrics in the BRD are targets and are not observed results.
