# Success metric plan

The BRD proposes manual-effort reduction 40%, ≥95% corrected-record pass rate, ≥80% mapping efficiency, 100% duplicate blocking under configured rules, 100% actionable invalid-record errors, ≥99% eligible-record processing reliability, and 100% traceability. These are **proposed targets, not measured outcomes**. Baseline, measurement method, population, and pilot approval are outstanding.
